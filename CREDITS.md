# Credits

## BuildCraft

BuildCraft Reborn is a port of [BuildCraft](https://github.com/BuildCraft/BuildCraft), created by SpaceToad and developed by the BuildCraft Team. This port is unofficial; please don't report its bugs to the BuildCraft Team.

| Part of BuildCraft | License | Where it applies here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mod code (`common/`) | MPL-2.0 | Java files ported from BuildCraft keep their header and stay MPL-2.0 |
| BuildCraft 7.1's robots (7.1's `common/buildcraft/robotics/`, and the 1.12 branch's `src_old_license/buildcraft/{robotics,silicon,core}/`) | MMPL 1.0 | Kept under the MMPL: the robot code in `robotics/` (entity, AIs, boards, stations, statements, crops, world properties, path finding, tables and requester) and the construction mark (`builders/tile/TileConstructionMarker`, `builders/block/BlockConstructionMarker`) keep the MMPL header (the licence text is in [LICENSE-ASSETS](LICENSE-ASSETS)) |
| BuildCraft 7.1's recipe packager, stamping table and package (7.1's `common/buildcraft/silicon/`) | MMPL 1.0 | Kept under the MMPL: `silicon/BCSiliconPackaging`, the packager and stamping table blocks, tiles, containers and GUIs, the package item, its tooltip and the thrown package keep the MMPL header |
| Filler patterns `PatternBox`, `PatternClear`, `PatternFill`, `PatternFrame`, `PatternPyramid`, `PatternStairs` | MMPL 1.0.1 | Kept under the MMPL: the six ported files in `builders/snapshot/pattern/` keep 1.12's MMPL header (the licence text is in [LICENSE-ASSETS](LICENSE-ASSETS)) |

Code ported so far:

- `buildcraftreborn.api` from `BuildCraftAPI/` (MIT, see [LICENSE-API](LICENSE-API)): the MJ power API, tile capabilities, core interfaces, engines, fuels and coolants, area and path providers, fluid shards, the transport (pipe, pluggable, wire and stripes) API, gate statements, facades and recipes.
- `buildcraftreborn.lib` from `common/buildcraft/lib/` (MPL-2.0): the expression language, base block and tile, network payloads, item handlers, tanks, fluids, delta values, the GUI base and ledgers, engines, markers and laser rendering.
- `buildcraftreborn.core` from `common/buildcraft/core/` (MPL-2.0): the wrench, engines, springs, markers, the marker connector, fluid shards and decorations.
- `buildcraftreborn.energy` from `common/buildcraft/energy/` (MPL-2.0): the stirling and combustion engines, the oil and fuel fluids, oil generation and the oil spring.
- `buildcraftreborn.factory` from `common/buildcraft/factory/` (MPL-2.0): the tank, mining well, pump, tube, flood gate, chute, auto workbench, distiller, heat exchanger and water gel, their GUIs and renderers, and the refinery recipe registry.
- `buildcraftreborn.transport` from `common/buildcraft/transport/` (MPL-2.0): the pipe holder, the 47 pipe types with their flows and behaviours, pipe models and rendering, wires, the blocker and power adapter, stripes handlers, the filtered buffer, the pipe GUIs, and the pipe triggers and actions.
- `buildcraftreborn.silicon` from `common/buildcraft/silicon/` (MPL-2.0): the laser and the assembly, advanced crafting and integration tables, gates and their GUI, lenses and filters, the pulsar, light sensor and timer, facades, chipsets, the gate copier, and their models and renderers.
- `buildcraftreborn.builders` from `common/buildcraft/builders/` (MPL-2.0, apart from the six MMPL patterns above): the quarry and its frame, the filler and its patterns and parameters, the architect table, builder, electronic library and replacer, blueprints, templates and single schematics, snapshots and their storage, schematic rules, the filler planner addon, and their GUIs, previews and renderers. The builders API (`api/filler`, `api/schematics`, `api/template` and related enums) is from `BuildCraftAPI/` (MIT).
- `buildcraftreborn.robotics` from `common/buildcraft/robotics/` (MPL-2.0): the zone planner, its map data, GUI and screen renderer, and zone plans. The rest of it is BuildCraft 7.1's robotics (MMPL 1.0), ported from the 1.12 branch's `src_old_license/` copy and 7.1 itself: the robot entity, its 40 AIs and 17 boards, docking stations, robot and station statements, crop handlers, world properties, path finding and block scanners, the robot registry, the requester, and the programming and charging tables (7.1's silicon). The robot API (`api/robots`, `api/boards`, `api/crops`, `api/events`, the programming recipe API) is from 7.1's `api/buildcraft/api/` (MIT). The robot's renderer, the item and station models and the JEI categories are new.
- The guide book engine (`lib/client/guide/`) from `common/buildcraft/lib/client/guide/` (MPL-2.0), with the guide item, the search suffix array (`lib/misc/search`) and the guide GUI helpers.
- The list (`core/item/ItemList_BC8`, `core/list`, `lib/list`), map location (`core/item/ItemMapLocation`) and debugger (`lib/item/ItemDebugger`), and the RF engine and MJ dynamo (`energy/tile`, `energy/client/gui`), from the matching `common/buildcraft/` files (MPL-2.0).
- `buildcraftreborn.compat` from BuildCraftCompat's `common/buildcraft/compat/module/` (MMPL 1.0.1): the JEI plugin, categories and transfer handlers, the Jade (1.12: Hwyla) and The One Probe providers, and the CraftTweaker assembly table, refinery and combustion engine support, all rewritten for those mods' 1.21.1 APIs. The EMI support (`compat/emi`) is new, as EMI didn't exist for 1.12, and follows the JEI plugin: its plugin class keeps BuildCraftCompat's MMPL, and its recipe and handler classes are MPL-2.0 like the JEI categories they follow. What both viewers show is built in `compat/viewer` (MMPL where it came out of the JEI plugin, MPL-2.0 otherwise).
- The paintbrush (`core/item/ItemPaintbrush_BC8`), core gate statements (`core/statements`), JSON variable models (`lib/client/model`), statement GUI elements (`lib/gui/statement`) and the assembly and integration recipe registries (`lib/recipe`) are ported from the matching `common/buildcraft/` files (MPL-2.0).
- The unit tests in `src/test/java` from BuildCraft's `src/test/java` (packet buffer, box, snapshot index and NBT tests) and `sub_projects/expression/src/test/java` (MPL-2.0). The filler pattern GameTests follow 1.12's `ShapePatternsTester`.

## This port

- The logo (`src/main/resources/logo.png`, shown in the mods list and the README) is new for BuildCraft Reborn and was supplied by the project's author. It isn't BuildCraft's art.

## Optional integrations

- [JEI](https://github.com/mezz/JustEnoughItems) by mezz (MIT), [EMI](https://github.com/emilyploszaj/emi) by Emi (MIT), [Jade](https://github.com/Snownee/Jade) by Snownee (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), [The One Probe](https://github.com/McJtyMods/TheOneProbe) by McJty (MIT) and [CraftTweaker](https://github.com/CraftTweaker/CraftTweaker) by BlameJared (MIT). Their APIs are only compiled against; none of their code is in BuildCraft Reborn's jar.

- [PneumaticCraft: Repressurized](https://github.com/TeamPneumatic/pnc-repressurized) by desht (GPL-3.0) is only downloaded for the optional cross-mod GameTests (`-Pinterop_runtime=true`); none of its code or assets are in this mod.
