<lore>
A little flying helper that does one job, and does it until its battery runs out.
</lore>
<no_lore>
A Robot works on its own: what it does depends on the Redstone Board inside it.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Getting a robot to work"/>
A robot fresh from the crafting table has no board and does nothing. Put a programmed board in it in an Integration Table: the robot comes out with that board, and with a fifth of a charge if it had none.
<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftreborn:robot"/>
Right click a Docking Station with the robot to place it. That station becomes its home: the robot flies off to work from there, comes back to sleep when it has nothing to do, and goes to a station on a kinesis pipe to recharge when its battery gets low.

<chapter name="Tools and areas"/>
Robots that need a tool (an axe, a pickaxe, a sword...) fetch one from a station whose gate provides items. Without a work area they look for work a long way around them; gate actions on their home station can give them an area from a Zone Planner map, and filters for what to work on.
<chapter name="Power"/>
A robot holds 10,000 MJ. It uses power as it works and flies, and gives off red puffs as it does. Charge it at a Docking Station on a kinesis pipe, or as an item in a Charging Table.
<chapter name="Taking it back"/>
Sneak and right click a robot with a wrench to turn it back into an item, with its board and its power. A robot can also wear a helmet or a head: right click it with one.
