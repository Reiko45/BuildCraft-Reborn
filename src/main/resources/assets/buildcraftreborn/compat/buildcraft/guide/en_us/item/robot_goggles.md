<no_lore>
Robot Goggles are worn on your head. While you wear them, every robot near you glows through walls.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Battery"/>
A robot's glow shows how charged it is: red when its battery is empty, yellow when it's half full and green when it's full. A red robot will soon be heading home to a Docking Station on a kinesis pipe to recharge, or has stopped.

<chapter name="Laser beams"/>
If laser beams are turned off in the client config, you still see them while wearing the goggles.
<chapter name="Protection"/>
The goggles don't protect you, and they never wear out. A robot can wear them too: right click it with them.
