<lore>
Every robot needs somewhere to come home to.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Docking Station is a pipe pluggable where robots are placed, dock, sleep and recharge.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Placing robots"/>
Put a Docking Station on the side of a pipe, then right click it with a programmed robot. The robot is linked to that station: it's its home.
<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftreborn:robot_station"/>
<chapter name="Lights"/>
The station's lights show whether it's free, reserved by a robot on its way, or the home of a robot.
<chapter name="Gates"/>
A gate on the same pipe controls the station. The station can provide items or fluids from the block the pipe pulls from (with a wooden pipe), accept items or fluids into the pipe, request items, ask robots for what the machine next to it needs (a Requester), give robots a work area, and forbid or force robots.
<chapter name="Power"/>
A station on a kinesis pipe charges the robot docked at it with the pipe's power.
