<chapter name="Programming"/>
Boards are made blank. Put a blank board in a Programming Table and pick a program; lasers then power the table until the board is ready. Green boards (picking up and carrying) are cheapest, blue boards (farming, forestry and mining) cost more, and red and yellow boards (fighting, bombing and stripes) cost the most.
