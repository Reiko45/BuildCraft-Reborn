A Package holds the ingredients of a crafting recipe, each in its slot of the crafting grid.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Making packages"/>
The Recipe Packager makes packages from paper, and a Stamping Table crafts them. The tooltip shows what a package holds, laid out as its crafting grid.
<chapter name="Throwing"/>
Use a package to throw it. Dispensers and stripes pipes throw them too. Where it lands, the package bursts open and drops what it holds, and anything it hits takes a little damage for each item inside.
