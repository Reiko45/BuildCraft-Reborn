<no_lore>
The Stamping Table is a laser table that crafts packages, and single items, with laser power.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Use"/>
Put a package in the slot on the left and point lasers at the table. It crafts the package's contents as a crafting grid, using 40 MJ for each item in it, and puts the result in the slot on the right.
Any other item is crafted on its own, as a one-item recipe: a log into planks, for example, for 40 MJ.
<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftreborn:stamping_table"/>
<chapter name="What's left over"/>
What can't be crafted moves to the output slot unchanged. What a recipe leaves behind, such as an empty bucket, goes in the three small slots, then into an inventory next to the table, then onto the ground.
<chapter name="Automation"/>
Pipes put packages and items in from any side, and take the results and leftovers out.
