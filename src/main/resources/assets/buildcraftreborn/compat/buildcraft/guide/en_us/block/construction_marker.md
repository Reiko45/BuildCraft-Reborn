Builders are big. Sometimes all you need is a marker and a few robots.
</lore>
<no_lore>
The Construction Mark holds a blueprint for builder robots to build.
</no_lore>
<chapter name="Use"/>
Place the mark, then right click it with a blueprint. The blueprint is built as a Builder in the mark's place would build it, facing the way you faced when you placed the mark: a short laser shows which way that is, and the blueprint's box is outlined. Sneak and right click with an empty hand (or a wrench) to take the blueprint back.
<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftreborn:construction_marker"/>
<chapter name="Builder robots"/>
Robots with the Builder board find the nearest mark that still has building to do. They fetch the items it needs from Docking Stations that provide items, fly them over, and give the mark power from their own batteries; the mark then clears and places the blocks. A mark can also be powered by engines.
